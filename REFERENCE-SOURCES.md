# Narendra Modi Stadium reference provenance

This file defines which public sources may drive Motera 3D architectural geometry. It exists to prevent visually similar or mislabeled stadium imagery from being used as Narendra Modi Stadium evidence.

## Venue identity

Every architectural reference used for geometry must explicitly identify **Narendra Modi Stadium / Motera Stadium in Ahmedabad, Gujarat**. A search-engine image caption, social repost, generic stadium article, or visually similar venue is not sufficient evidence by itself.

## Approved primary/project sources

### Gujarat Cricket Association (venue owner/operator)
- https://gujaratcricketassociation.com/narendra-modi-stadium/
- https://gujaratcricketassociation.com/msgallery/gca-stadium-gallery/
- https://gujaratcricketassociation.com/virtual-tour/

Use for venue identity, field dimensions, owner-published facilities, official gallery/visual context and capacity statements made by GCA.

### Populous (architect)
- https://populous.com/preview/narendra-modi-stadium
- https://populous.com/article/populous-designed-stadium-officially-inaugurated-by-world-leaders

Use for two-tier bowl concept, crowd/arrival strategy, elevated podium/north approach, sustainability and architectural design intent.

### Walter P Moore (roof structural engineer)
- https://www.walterpmoore.com/projects/narendra-modi-stadium-roof-design
- https://www.walterpmoore.com/news/howzat-for-worlds-largest-cricket-stadium
- https://www.walterpmoore.com/news/worlds-largest-cricket-stadium-opens-in-ahmedabad

Use for the cable-supported PTFE roof, approximately 30 m cantilever, single inner tension ring, bi-chord outer compression ring, radial ridge/valley cables, catwalk, V-column supports and structural separation from the concrete bowl.

### HIFAB (stadium facade contractor)
- https://www.hifab.in/projects/narendra-modi-stadium/

Use for the Ahmedabad stadium's three-dimensional eye-shaped aluminium-sheet facade and project identity. Do not infer exact panel dimensions, finish colour, location extent or curvature from the text alone.

### SSMB Motera construction article
- https://test2.ssmb.in/sardar-patel-motera-cricket-stadium-ahmedabad/

Use only to corroborate the doubly-curved facade construction: a 3D profile, bent steel-tube backing frame and segmented aluminium-sheet outer skin. Treat it as corroborating construction evidence, not an authoritative shop drawing.

### Mondo Stadia / project-team article hosted by Walter P Moore
- https://assets.walterpmoore.com/sites/default/files/mondo_stadia_-_motera_stadium.pdf

Use for documented project-team details such as the 580 Philips ArenaVision LED luminaires distributed around the stadium catwalk. Do not convert editorial diagrams or photos into exact surveyed geometry.

## Reference rules

1. Architectural geometry must be traceable to one or more sources above.
2. Search-engine image results are discovery aids only. They must not directly drive geometry unless the underlying page explicitly identifies the Ahmedabad stadium.
3. Similar venues, renderings for other stadiums, future Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel Sports Enclave buildings, and generic cricket-stadium imagery are forbidden as Narendra Modi Stadium geometry references.
4. Where sources support a **concept** but not dimensions, model the concept conservatively and label exact placement/dimensions as approximate.
5. Do not turn published capacity into invented row counts or official seat inventory. The current 109,910-chair procedural bowl is only a capacity calibration.
6. Do not claim facade shop geometry, roof engineering nodes, exact bowl rake/riser geometry, official Block/Bay/Row/Seat coordinates or surveyed site placement without authoritative drawings/data.
7. If two credible sources disagree, preserve the disagreement in documentation rather than silently choosing the visually convenient value.

## Phase 29 correction

Phase 29 removes two Phase 28 visual inventions introduced after ambiguous image matching:
- five generic sinusoidal facade ribbons are replaced by a segmented doubly-curved aluminium skin with tube backing;
- the invented tall outer roof crown is replaced by shallow bracing between the already-modeled bi-chord compression-ring levels;
- the continuous glowing roof ring is replaced by 580 distributed instanced catwalk luminaires.

These corrections improve provenance. They still do not make Motera 3D a CAD/BIM or survey-grade digital twin.
